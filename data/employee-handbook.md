# Larkspur Labs Employee Handbook

Larkspur Labs is a fictional 40-person company that builds soil sensors for vineyards. This handbook covers how we work.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 Central European Time. Outside core hours you can arrange your day freely. Meetings must not be scheduled outside core hours unless everyone invited agrees.

Fridays after 13:00 are "quiet time": no meetings, no non-urgent chat messages. Use it for deep work.

## Remote work

Everyone may work remotely up to three days per week. The Lisbon office is open Monday to Thursday. Team leads may ask for in-office days for planning weeks, which happen once per quarter and are announced at least four weeks ahead.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year. Longer stays need written approval from People Operations because of tax rules.

## Vacation and leave

Full-time employees get 27 vacation days per year, plus local public holidays. Unused vacation days carry over until March 31 of the following year and then expire.

Request vacation in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for anything longer than three days. Shorter breaks only need a heads-up to your team.

Sick leave does not require a doctor's note for the first three consecutive days. From the fourth day on, send a note to People Operations.

Parental leave is 16 weeks fully paid for all parents, which can be split into up to three blocks within the child's first two years.

## Equipment

Every new hire chooses a laptop from the approved list (currently a 14-inch MacBook Pro or a ThinkPad X1 Carbon). Laptops are replaced every three years.

There is a home-office budget of 600 euros per employee, available once every two years, for a desk, chair, monitor or similar items. Submit the receipt through the expenses tool.

## Expenses

Expenses under 50 euros need no pre-approval. Anything between 50 and 500 euros needs approval from your team lead. Above 500 euros, approval from the finance team is also required.

Travel is booked through the company travel tool. Train is preferred over flying for trips shorter than six hours.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 1,200 euros for courses, books and conferences, plus five learning days that do not count as vacation.
