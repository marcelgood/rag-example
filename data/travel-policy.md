# Larkspur Labs Travel Policy

Business travel at Larkspur Labs (a fictional company) is booked through the company travel tool. This policy explains what the company pays for. For approving the cost of a trip, the normal expense rules in the employee handbook apply.

## Getting there

Take the train for any trip shorter than six hours door to door. Flights are allowed for longer trips, always in economy class. Business class is never reimbursed, also not for long-haul flights.

Taxis and ride-hailing are fine between 22:00 and 06:00, when carrying equipment such as sensors or gateways, or when no public transport goes to a customer's vineyard. Otherwise use public transport.

Using your own car is reimbursed at 0.30 euros per kilometer. Parking and tolls are reimbursed on top, with receipts.

## Hotels

Book a standard room close to the customer or the venue. The maximum price per night is 140 euros in most cities, and 190 euros in London, Paris, Zurich and Munich. If no room is available under the limit, ask your team lead to approve an exception before booking.

## Meals and daily allowance

On travel days you receive a daily allowance (per diem) of 45 euros for meals, or 25 euros if the trip is shorter than eight hours. Meals paid by a customer or at a conference are deducted from the allowance. No receipts are needed for the allowance.

Alcohol is not reimbursed, except for one drink per person at a customer dinner approved by your team lead.

## Customer visits

When visiting vineyards, bring the safety boots from the Lisbon office equipment room; the company does not reimburse personal boots. Sensors and gateways for installations travel as checked luggage or are shipped ahead to the customer at least five working days before the visit.

## Submitting travel costs

Submit all travel costs through the expenses tool within 30 days of returning, with receipts for everything except the daily allowance.
