# Larkspur Labs Information Security Policy

This policy applies to every employee and contractor of Larkspur Labs (a fictional company). Questions go to the security team at security@larkspur.test.

## Accounts and passwords

Use the company password manager for every work account. Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique per service. Never reuse a work password anywhere else.

Two-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the code repository, the cloud console and the HR portal. Use the authenticator app or a hardware key; text messages are not accepted as a second factor.

Shared accounts are not allowed. If a tool only supports one login, ask the security team to set up delegated access instead.

## Laptops and devices

Company laptops must have full-disk encryption switched on, and the screen must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity. Operating system updates must be installed within 14 days of release.

Personal phones may be used for email and chat only after enrolling them in device management. Customer data must never be stored on personal devices.

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the security team within one hour, so that it can be wiped remotely.

## Phishing and suspicious messages

If an email or chat message asks you to log in, pay an invoice or share data unexpectedly, do not click any links. Forward it to security@larkspur.test and then delete it. Reporting a message you are unsure about is always welcome, even if it turns out to be harmless.

## Access to customer data

Access to production systems and customer data is granted per role and reviewed every quarter by the engineering manager. Access is removed on an employee's last working day.

Customer data may only be used to operate and support the service. Copying customer data into personal notes, spreadsheets or AI tools that are not approved by the security team is not allowed.

## Security incidents

A suspected data breach is always treated as a SEV1 incident under the on-call runbook. The security team decides within 24 hours whether customers and the data protection authority must be informed; the legal deadline for informing the authority is 72 hours.
