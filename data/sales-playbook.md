# Larkspur Labs Sales Playbook

How the sales team of Larkspur Labs (a fictional company) prices and closes deals. For list prices, see the product FAQ.

## Who we sell to

Our customers are vineyards and wine estates in Portugal, Spain, France, Italy and Germany with at least 5 hectares of vines. A typical vineyard needs one sensor per irrigation block, or about one sensor per hectare, plus one gateway.

## Discounts

- Orders of 50 sensors or more get a 10% discount on the sensors. The gateway and the cloud dashboard are never discounted.
- Customers who sign a three-year dashboard contract get the first three months of the dashboard for free.
- Any other discount needs approval from the head of sales, and no discount may exceed 20% of the order value.

Wine cooperatives buying for several member vineyards are treated as one customer, so their combined order counts toward the volume discount.

## Payment terms

Invoices are due 30 days after the invoice date. New customers with an order above 10,000 euros pay 50% upfront and the rest on delivery. An invoice that is 14 days overdue triggers a reminder from finance; after 45 days, the dashboard access of the customer is paused until the invoice is paid.

## Pilot projects

A vineyard can test the Rootline with a pilot kit of five sensors and one gateway for one growing season at a fixed price of 750 euros. If the customer places a full order within 60 days after the pilot, the pilot price is credited against the order.

## Resellers

Agricultural equipment dealers can resell the Rootline. Resellers receive a 15% margin on hardware and 10% on the first year of the dashboard subscription. Reseller agreements are signed by the head of sales.
