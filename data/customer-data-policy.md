# Rootline Customer Data Policy

How Larkspur Labs (a fictional company) stores and protects the data that Rootline customers collect. This summary is shared with customers on request.

## What we store

We store the readings of every sensor (moisture, temperature and electrical conductivity), the location of sensors and gateways, and the account details of the people who use the dashboard. We do not collect any data about vineyard workers or visitors.

## Where the data is stored

All data is stored in data centers in Frankfurt, Germany, and never leaves the European Union. Backups are made daily and kept for 35 days.

## How long we keep it

Sensor readings are kept for as long as the customer has an active dashboard subscription, so that customers can compare seasons. After a subscription ends, readings are kept for another 12 months, in case the customer returns, and are then deleted.

A customer can ask for all of their data to be deleted at any time. We delete it within 30 days of the request, including from backups.

## Who can see the data

Only the customer and the people they invite can see their readings. Larkspur Labs support staff can see a customer's data only to solve a support request, and every access is logged. We never sell customer data or share it with other customers.

Anonymized, aggregated readings, such as the average soil moisture per region, may be used to improve the product. They never identify a vineyard.

## Exporting data

Customers can export all readings as CSV from the dashboard at any time. On request, we also provide a full export of the account in JSON format within five working days.
