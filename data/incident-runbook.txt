LARKSPUR LABS — ON-CALL INCIDENT RUNBOOK (fictional)

Severity levels

SEV1: the cloud dashboard is down for all customers, or data is being lost. Page the on-call engineer immediately and open an incident channel named #inc-<date>-<short-name>. Post a status page update within 15 minutes.

SEV2: a major feature is broken for many customers, for example alerts are not being sent, but no data is lost. The on-call engineer responds within 30 minutes during core hours and within 2 hours outside them.

SEV3: a minor bug or a problem affecting a single customer. Create a ticket; it is handled during normal working hours.

Who is on call

On-call rotates weekly, starting Monday at 10:00. Each rotation has a primary and a secondary engineer. If the primary does not acknowledge a page within 10 minutes, it escalates automatically to the secondary, and after another 10 minutes to the engineering manager.

On-call engineers receive a flat allowance of 250 euros per week, plus time off in lieu for any incident work done at night or on weekends.

After an incident

Every SEV1 and SEV2 incident gets a blameless postmortem, written within five working days. The postmortem lists the timeline, the root cause, and follow-up actions with an owner for each. Postmortems are shared with the whole company.
