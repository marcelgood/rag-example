# Lisbon Office Guide

Practical information about the Larkspur Labs (a fictional company) office in Lisbon, at Rua das Vinhas 42, 3rd floor.

## Access

The office is open Monday to Thursday from 08:00 to 19:00. Your access badge opens the street door and the office door. Lost badges are replaced by the office manager for a fee of 15 euros.

Visitors must be signed in at reception and accompanied at all times. Customers visiting for a demo are welcome to use the meeting room "Douro" without booking ahead.

## Wi-Fi

The employee network is called LARKSPUR-STAFF and uses your normal company login. Guests use LARKSPUR-GUEST; the password changes every Monday and is posted at reception.

## Desks and meeting rooms

All desks are shared. Book a desk in the office app before you come in, at the latest by 09:00 on the day. The two meeting rooms, "Douro" and "Alentejo", are booked through the company calendar for up to two hours at a time.

## Food and drinks

Coffee, tea and fruit are free. On Wednesdays the company pays for a team lunch for everyone in the office. There are three fridges; anything left in them is thrown away on Thursday afternoon.

## Getting here

The office is a five-minute walk from the Cais do Sodré metro and train station. There is no car parking, but the building has a locked bicycle room on the ground floor; ask the office manager for access.

## Safety

The fire assembly point is the square in front of the building. A fire drill is held twice a year. The first-aid kit and the defibrillator are next to the kitchen.

## Equipment room

The equipment room next to reception holds demo sensors, two demo gateways, installation augers and safety boots in all sizes for customer visits. Sign items out in the office app and return them within one week.
