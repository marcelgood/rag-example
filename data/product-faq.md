# Rootline Sensor — Product FAQ

The Rootline is the soil sensor made by Larkspur Labs (a fictional company).

## What does the Rootline measure?

Soil moisture at three depths (10 cm, 30 cm and 60 cm), soil temperature, and electrical conductivity, which indicates salt and nutrient levels. Readings are taken every 15 minutes.

## How long does the battery last?

The sealed lithium battery lasts about five years with the default 15-minute interval. Switching to a 5-minute interval reduces battery life to roughly two years. The battery cannot be replaced by the customer; the whole sensor is swapped under the battery replacement program.

## How does the sensor send data?

The Rootline uses LoRaWAN radio. One Rootline Gateway covers up to 3 km in open vineyard terrain and can serve up to 200 sensors. The gateway connects to the internet over Ethernet or 4G.

## What happens if the connection drops?

Each sensor stores up to 30 days of readings locally and uploads them automatically once the connection comes back.

## How do I install a sensor?

Use the included auger to drill a 70 cm hole, insert the sensor, and backfill with the original soil. Pair it with the Rootline app by scanning the QR code on the cap. Installation takes about ten minutes per sensor.

## What is the warranty?

Two years for sensors and gateways. Damage from machinery, such as tractors or mowers, is not covered.

## How much does it cost?

A Rootline sensor costs 189 euros. The gateway costs 449 euros. The cloud dashboard subscription is 4 euros per sensor per month, billed yearly.
