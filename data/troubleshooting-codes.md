# Rootline Troubleshooting: Error Codes

Rootline sensors report problems as error codes in the cloud dashboard and the Rootline app. This list is maintained by Larkspur Labs (a fictional company) support.

## Sensor error codes

- **ERR-01 Low battery.** The sensor has less than 10% battery left, which is usually a few months. Request a replacement under the battery replacement program.
- **ERR-03 No gateway in range.** The sensor has not reached a gateway for six hours. Readings are kept on the sensor for up to 30 days. Check that the gateway is powered and online, or move the gateway closer.
- **ERR-07 Moisture probe fault.** One of the three moisture probes returns impossible values. This is usually caused by an air gap around the sensor after installation. Reinstall the sensor and press the soil firmly around it. If the error stays after 24 hours, request a replacement.
- **ERR-09 Water ingress.** Moisture was detected inside the housing. Remove the sensor and contact support; it is replaced free of charge within the warranty.
- **ERR-12 Clock drift.** The sensor's clock is more than five minutes off. It corrects itself at the next contact with a gateway. No action is needed unless the error repeats daily.

## Gateway error codes

- **GW-21 Firmware update failed.** The gateway keeps running the previous firmware and retries the next night. If it fails three nights in a row, restart the gateway.
- **GW-30 Too many sensors.** More than 200 sensors are paired with one gateway. Add a second gateway; the extra sensors are not lost and pair with the new gateway automatically.

## Returning a device

To return a faulty sensor or gateway, open a return request (RMA) in the Rootline app. Support replies within two working days with a prepaid shipping label. Replacements are shipped as soon as the faulty device has been scanned at our warehouse.
